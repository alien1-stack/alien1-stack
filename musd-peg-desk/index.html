Quoting the MUSD/BTC pool…

Redeem arb: buy MUSD with BTC on the Mezo MUSD/BTC pool, redeem it for BTC at $1 minus the redemption fee. Mint-and-sell: borrow MUSD (borrowing fee) and sell it on the pool; interest and locked collateral not included. Pool quotes include the pool fee and price impact.

Borrower Defense

Redemptions take BTC from the loans at the front of the queue, at $1 per MUSD. Check any MUSD loan: how much MUSD must be redeemed before it is hit, and what moves it back. Read-only.

Redemption queue

Redemptions take BTC from the loan with the lowest collateral ratio at or above 110% first, in this exact on-chain order (SortedTroves). "MUSD ahead" is how much must be redeemed before that loan is touched.
